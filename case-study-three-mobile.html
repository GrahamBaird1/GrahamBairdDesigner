Case study 01

Website design for an international telecommunications organisation

Overseeing the UX design and daily management of the UK website for Three Mobile across 12 months — ensuring the site remained current, functional, and commercially effective at all times.

Project objectivesMaintain and evolve the Three Mobile UK website to support commercial campaigns, improve user experience, and ensure content remained accurate, accessible, and on-brand throughout the engagement.

What I did

The challenge

Three Mobile required a designer who could work at pace within an agile environment — managing multiple concurrent workstreams, responding to commercial priorities, and maintaining design quality across a high-traffic consumer website.

My approach

Working directly within the digital product team, I established a clear workflow for receiving briefs, translating business requirements into user-centred designs, and delivering production-ready assets. I maintained close communication with developers, marketing, and brand stakeholders throughout.

Each new page or feature was approached through rapid wireframing and prototyping, with designs validated against existing user data before handoff. Content was written to GDS-aligned plain language standards, ensuring clarity for all users regardless of technical literacy.

The outcome
