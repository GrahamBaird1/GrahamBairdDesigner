What I do

One design discipline. Multiple perspectives.

The best digital experiences don't come from treating UX, UI, content and service design as separate activities. I bring them together.

UX design

Understanding people, their needs and the problems standing in their way — then designing experiences around them.

UI & interaction design

Turning journeys and ideas into clear, intuitive interfaces that help people understand what they can do and what happens next.

Content design

Making information easier to understand through purposeful content, structure, language and interaction.

Service design

Looking beyond the interface to understand the wider service, its people, processes and touchpoints.

Research & discovery

Using research, workshops, interviews and prototyping to understand problems before committing to solutions.

My approach

Understand. Simplify. Validate.

01

Understand

I start with the problem, not the solution. I explore the needs of users, the organisation and the wider service to understand what is really happening and where the opportunities lie.

02

Simplify

Complexity rarely needs to be passed on to the person using the service. I turn research and insight into clearer journeys, interactions, interfaces and content.

03

Validate

Good ideas need evidence. I prototype, test, iterate and refine — using feedback to improve the experience before it reaches the people who depend on it.

About me

I'm Graham.

I'm a senior multidisciplinary digital designer based in the UK. I specialise in making complex things simpler — combining UX, UI, content and service design to create digital experiences that are clear, accessible and useful.

My career has taken me from commercial digital products and global brands to some of the UK's most complex public services. I've worked with organisations including the Ministry of Justice, GOV.UK, Scottish Government, Three, Hilton and the University of Glasgow, working with multidisciplinary teams to understand problems, explore opportunities and deliver better digital experiences.

That's the value of multidisciplinary design: seeing the whole experience, not just one part of it.
