Approach to work

Ensure it's informed and keep it simple

01

Understand before designing

I start with research — interviews, contextual enquiry, data analysis. Design decisions are only as good as the understanding behind them.

02

Design the system, not the screen

Individual screens are symptoms. I design the underlying logic: the flows, the states, the edge cases, the language — so the whole coheres.

03

Prototype to learn, not to sell

Low-fidelity early, interactive fast. Prototypes are questions made tangible — not polished deliverables to be defended.

04

Ship with precision

Close collaboration with engineering at every stage. I care about what ships, not just what's in Figma — including the words on screen.

About me

Hi, I'm Graham...

Your photo

I'm a UX designer with over a decade of experience working on complex, high-stakes digital products — government services, healthcare platforms, financial tools, and enterprise SaaS.

I'm a multidisciplinary, user-centred designer specialising in:

  • UX design
  • Service design
  • Content design
  • and more...

My background spans the complete design lifecycle: from stakeholder alignment and discovery, through information architecture and interaction design, to design system governance and post-launch iteration.

I'm drawn to problems where the gap between user expectation and product reality is large — and where good design can close it meaningfully. I care about clarity: in how a product works, how it feels, and how it speaks.

Previously at HMRC, NHS Digital, and two venture-backed startups. Now available for senior contract and fractional engagements.
